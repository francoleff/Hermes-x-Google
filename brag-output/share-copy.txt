Your Google work, without the tab hunt. Google Workspace for Hermes puts Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts in the Hermes sidebar and gives the agent 38 tools to work across them, like "prepare me for my meeting with James tomorrow" turning into a briefing Doc. Reads are automatic; sends and shares wait for your approval.
